Scone The Orange Cat

Introduction

Scone was found in The University of Fiji, near the steps of the Saweni Campus. He was following around a female black cat. Feintwell was taking pictures of the black beauty when a nursing student had approached and grabbed Scone. Feintwell was oblivious to Scone until that moment, and the kind girl told Feintwell that the kittens of the university can be taken home. There was hearts in Feintwell's eyes as she claimed the orange kitten. The kind girl took a black kitten, both were put in a box in the bookshop for the rest of the day. At 4, after both the women had finished their classes, they came to get the 2 kittens. The black kitten was taken by the girl in her hands and her parents picked her from the university campus. Feintwell took the box with the docile orange kitten to the bus, for an hour and as half ride home. The kitten was wrapped in Feintwell's jacket in the box and practically slept the whole ride to his new home
